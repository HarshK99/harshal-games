🪂 Squad Royale

Your squad of 5 vs 2 enemy squads. Last squad standing wins the chicken dinner.

Choose your character

The computer fills the rest of your 5-player squad, plus 2 full enemy squads.

Spectating –
Move: WASD or arrow keys. Aim with your mouse, click (or hold) to shoot. Buildings block bullets — use them as cover. Stay inside the shrinking blue zone or you'll take damage. On phone: drag to move, auto-fires at the nearest enemy. If you're eliminated, you'll spectate your last teammate.
