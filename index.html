Harshal Games App

A collection of small builds — character sheets, games, and computer vision experiments
🏏

Character Sheet

Harshal Jain, all-rounder fast bowler — stats, bars, and a click-counter mini-game.
✋

Hand Cricket

Classic hand cricket vs the computer — play by tapping numbers or with live hand-gesture detection.
⭕

Circle Challenge

Draw a circle in the air with your finger. Two players, three tries each, scored on roundness.
🖐️

Gesture Detector

Standalone test bed for the finger-counting computer vision used by the other apps.
